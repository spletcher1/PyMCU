# OptoTest 7: Progressive ratio counting time in contact, the default for ratio Paradigms
# from PyMCU 4.0.1. Each Activation needs more milliseconds of contact than the last. Once the
# Requirement is met, the LED lights for exactly ActivationMs.
#
# What to expect: hold a Well. After 1 s of contact its LED lights for 1 s (FirstRequirement
# 1000, ActivationMs 1000); holding while it is lit doesn't count. Keep holding: the next light
# comes after 2 s more contact, then 3 s, 4 s and so on, up to 10 s each (Cap 10000). Contact
# adds up across touches with no timeout, so two half-second touches earn the first light as
# one 1-second touch does. The data file shows each Well's Requirement and Progress in ms.
#
# Settings: with Counter: Contact, FirstRequirement, Step and Cap are ms of contact, multiples
# of 10, up to 655350; the DFM counts in steps of 10 ms. Series Exponential (Scale in ms, and
# Rate) is the Richardson and Roberts series instead. Counter: Contact is the default and is
# written out here only to show it; OptoTest 5 counts feeding bouts with Counter: Events.
#
# Needs DFM firmware 4.0.1. PyMCU refuses it for older boards, 4.0.0 included, which would
# count bouts instead. ID: All makes every connected DFM run it.
